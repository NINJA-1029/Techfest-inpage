# 45-second demo walkthrough

1. Start at `index.html` and introduce the 15 concepts as three distinct web experiences for each of five visual directions.
2. Open **Gravity Lab**. Move the pointer over the hero to tilt the CSS-built spatial form; scroll through festival areas and the closing CTA.
3. Return to the gallery and open **Signal Drift**. Scroll to show the layered scene moving at different speeds.
4. Open **Impact Index** to show the flat type and graphic shapes, with no 3D assets.
5. End back at the concept gallery. Keep this narration factual: these are concept pages, not an official redesign or live event schedule.
