// 05 · CLARIFICATIONS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS.

Verified event essentials and official pathways. For unconfirmed schedules or specific rules, refer to official IIT Bombay portals.

Techfest is the annual science and technology festival organized entirely by the student body of the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Bombay. Celebrated as Asia's premier science and technology festival, it attracts over 175,000 attendees annually, providing an open arena for competitive robotics, international research showcases, masterclasses, and keynote dialogues with world-renowned scientists.
Techfest ’26 is officially scheduled for 16–18 December 2026. The entire festival is hosted on-campus at the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay, nestled between Powai Lake and the Sanjay Gandhi National Park hills in Powai, Mumbai, Maharashtra (Coordinates: 19.1334° N, 72.9133° E).
All official problem statements, technical constraints, team composition rules, prize distributions, and workshop curricula are published directly and exclusively on the official Techfest website (techfest.org). We recommend checking the official portal regularly as problem statements are progressively released throughout the autumn semester.
Historically, general admission to view the robotics arenas, scientific exhibitions, and keynote talks is complimentary for registered students holding valid institutional identification. Specific intensive hands-on workshops and competition entry slots involve official portal registration. Always verify pricing and register directly at techfest.org.
